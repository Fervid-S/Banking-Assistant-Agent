GOLD LOAN — PRODUCT TERMS, ELIGIBILITY, AND CHARGES

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
The Bank's Gold Loan scheme provides secured credit against the pledge of gold jewellery and ornaments. Gold coins sold by banks (up to 50 grams) are also accepted as collateral. Gold bars, biscuits purchased from sources other than banks, and hallmarked bullion are not eligible for pledge. The product is suitable for meeting short-term liquidity requirements including agricultural expenses, business working capital, medical emergencies, and education-related expenditure.

ELIGIBILITY
  • Any resident individual aged 18 years or above.
  • KYC compliance is mandatory. Customers must present valid Aadhaar and PAN.
  • Joint ownership of pledged gold is permitted; all owners must be present at the branch and provide consent.
  • Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) are not eligible under this scheme.

GOLD PURITY AND VALUATION
  • Only gold jewellery of 18 carat and above purity is accepted.
  • The Bank's empanelled appraisers assess purity at the branch using acid test or XRF spectrometry.
  • Valuation is computed at the Bank's declared Gold Rate for the day, which is derived from the average of the London Bullion Market Association (LBMA) spot rate and the domestic multi-commodity exchange (MCX) rate. The Bank reserves the right to revise this rate daily.
  • Stones, beads, and non-gold components embedded in jewellery are excluded from valuation.

LOAN PARAMETERS
  • Minimum loan amount: ₹10,000.
  • Maximum loan amount: ₹25,00,000 per customer across all Gold Loan accounts.
  • Maximum LTV: 75% of the appraised gold value, as mandated by the Reserve Bank of India.
  • Loan tenure: Minimum 3 months; maximum 12 months.

INTEREST RATES AND SCHEMES
The Bank offers three Gold Loan variants:

  1. Bullet Repayment Scheme: Interest and principal are repaid in a single bullet payment at maturity. Interest rate: 9.50% per annum. Suitable for customers with irregular income (farmers, traders).

  2. Monthly Interest Scheme: Borrower pays interest monthly; principal is repaid at maturity. Interest rate: 9.75% per annum.

  3. EMI Scheme: Both principal and interest are repaid in equal monthly instalments. Interest rate: 10.00% per annum (reducing balance).

PROCESSING CHARGES
  • Processing fee: ₹250 flat for loans up to ₹1,00,000; ₹500 flat for loans above ₹1,00,000.
  • Appraisal fee: Nil (covered within processing fee).
  • Stamp duty: As applicable per state government regulations.

RENEWAL AND AUCTION
  • Gold Loans may be renewed at maturity upon payment of accrued interest.
  • In the event of default, the Bank will issue a 14-day notice before initiating auction proceedings.
  • Auction is conducted through empanelled auctioneers. Any surplus after recovery of dues (principal + interest + charges) is remitted to the borrower.

CLOSURE AND GOLD RELEASE
  • Upon full repayment, pledged gold is released within 2 working hours at the branch.
  • Borrowers must collect their gold within 30 days of loan closure; uncollected gold is held in the Bank's vault and a custodian charge of ₹100 per month may apply.

Note: LTV norms are governed by the Reserve Bank of India Master Directions on Loans Against Gold and are subject to change without prior notice.
