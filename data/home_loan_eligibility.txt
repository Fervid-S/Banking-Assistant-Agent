HOME LOAN — PRODUCT TERMS AND ELIGIBILITY GUIDELINES

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
The Bank's Home Loan product facilitates the purchase of ready-to-move or under-construction residential properties, construction of a house on owned land, and renovation or extension of existing residential premises. Loans for the acquisition of commercial properties are not covered under this scheme.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Salaried Applicants:
  • Age: Minimum 21 years at the time of application; maximum 65 years at loan maturity.
  • Minimum monthly net income: ₹25,000.
  • Employment type: Confirmed/permanent employees of Central or State Government, PSUs, or corporates with a credit rating of BBB and above.
  • Minimum service: 2 years of continuous employment, with at least 1 year in the current organisation.

Self-Employed Applicants:
  • Age: Minimum 25 years; maximum 70 years at loan maturity.
  • Minimum net annual income: ₹3,00,000 (as per last 2 years' ITR).
  • Business vintage: Minimum 3 years in the same line of business.
  • Professionals (doctors, CAs, architects): Minimum 2 years post-qualification practice.

LOAN PARAMETERS
  • Minimum loan amount: ₹5,00,000.
  • Maximum loan amount: ₹5,00,00,000 (subject to eligibility and property valuation).
  • Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio:
      – Up to ₹30,00,000: Maximum 90% of property value.
      – ₹30,00,001 to ₹75,00,000: Maximum 80% of property value.
      – Above ₹75,00,000: Maximum 75% of property value.
  • Maximum tenure: 30 years for salaried applicants; 20 years for self-employed applicants. Tenure may not extend beyond the applicant's 70th birthday (65th for salaried).

INTEREST RATES
Home Loans are offered on a floating interest rate basis linked to the Bank's Repo Linked Lending Rate (RLLR). The applicable rate as of the last revision:
  • RLLR: 9.15% per annum.
  • Spread for salaried (CIBIL ≥750): RLLR + 0.00% = 9.15% per annum.
  • Spread for salaried (CIBIL 700–749): RLLR + 0.25% = 9.40% per annum.
  • Spread for self-employed (CIBIL ≥750): RLLR + 0.50% = 9.65% per annum.
  • Spread for self-employed (CIBIL below 700): Not eligible under standard scheme.

PROCESSING AND OTHER CHARGES
  • Processing fee: 0.50% of the loan amount, minimum ₹3,000, maximum ₹15,000. Non-refundable.
  • Prepayment: No prepayment penalty for floating rate home loans, in accordance with RBI guidelines.
  • Late payment charge: 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount.
  • Technical and legal report charges: ₹5,000 (urban/metro properties), ₹3,000 (rural/semi-urban).

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED (SALARIED)
  • Completed application form with passport-size photographs.
  • PAN card and Aadhaar card (OVD).
  • Last 6 months' bank statement of the salary account.
  • Last 3 months' salary slips.
  • Form 16 (last 2 years).
  • Property title documents and approved building plan.

Note: The Bank reserves the right to obtain additional documentation at its discretion. Final sanction is subject to the Bank's credit appraisal process and property due diligence.
