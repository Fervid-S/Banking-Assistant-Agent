CREDIT CARD VARIANTS — FEATURES, FEES, AND CHARGES

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
The Bank issues co-branded and proprietary credit cards across Visa and Mastercard networks. All credit cards operate on a revolving credit basis. Interest is charged only on amounts not cleared in full by the payment due date. The minimum amount due is calculated as 5% of the outstanding balance or ₹200, whichever is higher.

CARD VARIANTS

1. CLASSIC CREDIT CARD (Entry-Level)
  • Eligibility: Minimum annual gross income of ₹2,50,000 (salaried or self-employed).
  • Joining fee: ₹500 (waived if annual spend exceeds ₹25,000 in the first year).
  • Annual fee: ₹500 (waived if annual spend exceeds ₹50,000).
  • Credit limit: ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000 (basis credit score and income).
  • Reward points: 1 point per ₹100 spent. Points redeemable against statement credit at 25 paise per point.
  • Fuel surcharge waiver: 1% (for transactions between ₹400 and ₹4,000; capped at ₹250 per statement cycle).
  • Interest-free period: Up to 50 days.

2. SIGNATURE CREDIT CARD (Mid-Range)
  • Eligibility: Minimum annual gross income of ₹6,00,000.
  • Joining fee: ₹1,500 (waived if annual spend exceeds ₹60,000 in the first year).
  • Annual fee: ₹1,500 (waived if annual spend exceeds ₹1,20,000).
  • Credit limit: ₹1,00,000 to ₹5,00,000.
  • Reward points: 2 points per ₹100 on all categories; 5 points per ₹100 on dining and online shopping.
  • Complimentary airport lounge access: 2 visits per quarter at domestic lounges (Visa Signature network).
  • Complimentary golf rounds: 1 round per quarter at participating golf courses.
  • Interest-free period: Up to 50 days.

3. INFINITE CREDIT CARD (Premium)
  • Eligibility: Minimum annual gross income of ₹15,00,000 or existing relationship with Wealth Management.
  • Joining fee: ₹5,000 (non-waivable).
  • Annual fee: ₹5,000 (waived if annual spend exceeds ₹5,00,000).
  • Credit limit: ₹5,00,000 and above (no published upper limit; individually assessed).
  • Reward points: 5 points per ₹100 on all spends; 10 points per ₹100 on international transactions.
  • Airport lounge access: Unlimited domestic; 8 international lounge visits per year (Priority Pass).
  • Concierge services: 24×7 personal concierge assistance.
  • Comprehensive travel and medical insurance: Cover up to ₹1,00,00,000 for air accidents; ₹10,00,000 for medical emergencies abroad.

COMMON CHARGES ACROSS ALL VARIANTS
  • Finance charge (interest): 3.40% per month (40.80% per annum) on the unpaid balance.
  • Late payment charge:
      – Outstanding up to ₹500: Nil.
      – ₹501 to ₹5,000: ₹300.
      – ₹5,001 to ₹10,000: ₹500.
      – Above ₹10,000: ₹750.
  • Cash advance fee: 2.50% of the cash advance amount, minimum ₹500.
  • Cash advance interest: Charged from the date of the transaction at 3.40% per month.
  • Foreign currency markup: 3.50% on the converted INR transaction value.
  • Over-limit fee: ₹600 per statement cycle (only if over-limit usage has been pre-authorised).
  • Card replacement fee: ₹200 (standard); ₹500 (emergency/express delivery).

EMI CONVERSION
  • Eligible transactions of ₹5,000 and above can be converted to EMI at the following rates:
      – 3 months: 12% per annum.
      – 6 months: 12% per annum.
      – 12 months: 13.50% per annum.
      – 24 months: 14% per annum.
  • Processing fee for EMI conversion: ₹199 per transaction.
