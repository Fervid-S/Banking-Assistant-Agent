BANK-ASSURANCE PRODUCTS — INSURANCE OFFERINGS THROUGH THE BANK

OVERVIEW
The Bank acts as a Corporate Agent for select life insurance and general insurance companies under the IRDAI (Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India) framework. The Bank does not underwrite insurance risk; all insurance products are issued by the partner insurance company. The Bank earns a referral commission; however, premium amounts and policy terms are governed exclusively by the partner insurer's product structure.

LIFE INSURANCE PRODUCTS

1. Term Life Insurance
  • Partner: Leading IRDAI-registered life insurance company.
  • Eligibility: Age 18 to 65 years; maximum maturity age 70 years.
  • Sum Assured: ₹25,00,000 to ₹5,00,00,000.
  • Policy Term: 10 to 35 years.
  • Premium indicative (non-smoker male, age 30, ₹1 crore, 30-year term): Approximately ₹9,000 per annum.
  • Riders available: Accidental Death Benefit, Critical Illness Rider (34 listed conditions), Waiver of Premium.
  • Payout: Sum Assured paid as lump sum to nominee on death of life assured during the policy term. No maturity benefit.

2. Endowment / Money-Back Plans
  • Guaranteed maturity benefit plus life cover.
  • Indicative premium for ₹5,00,000 sum assured, 20-year term: ₹23,000–₹27,000 per annum (varies by product variant and age).
  • Participating plans allow for annual bonuses declared by the insurer.

3. Unit Linked Insurance Plan (ULIP)
  • Dual benefit: Life cover + investment in market-linked funds (equity, debt, balanced).
  • Lock-in period: 5 years (IRDAI mandated).
  • Fund options: Large Cap, Mid Cap, Debt, Balanced, and Liquid funds.
  • Charges: Premium allocation charge (1–5%), policy administration charge, fund management charge (up to 1.35% per annum).
  • Note: ULIP returns are market-linked and not guaranteed. Past performance is not indicative of future returns.

GENERAL INSURANCE PRODUCTS

1. Motor Insurance (Two-Wheeler and Private Car)
  • Third-party (TP) cover: Mandatory as per the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988. Premium is IRDAI-regulated.
  • Comprehensive policy: Covers own damage plus third-party liability. Premium is based on Insured Declared Value (IDV), vehicle age, and add-ons.
  • No Claim Bonus (NCB): Discount on own-damage premium for every claim-free year (up to 50% after 5 consecutive claim-free years).

2. Health Insurance (Mediclaim)
  • Individual and family floater plans available.
  • Sum insured options: ₹3,00,000 to ₹1,00,00,000.
  • Indicative annual premium (family floater, ₹5 lakh SI, couple aged 35): ₹12,000–₹18,000.
  • Features: Cashless treatment at 8,000+ empanelled hospitals; ambulance cover; maternity cover (waiting period 2–4 years); restore benefit (automatic reinstatement of sum insured after depletion).
  • Pre-existing disease waiting period: Typically 2 to 4 years (varies by insurer).
  • Co-payment clause: Applicable for senior citizen policies (10–20% co-pay on each claim).

3. Home Insurance
  • Covers the structure of the residential property against fire, flood, earthquake, lightning, and allied perils.
  • Optional contents cover: Jewellery, electronics, furniture up to a specified limit.
  • Premium: Approximately ₹2,000–₹5,000 per annum for a ₹50 lakh property cover (varies by location and construction type).

Insurance products are offered by the Bank as a corporate agent. The Bank is not responsible for claims settlement, which is the sole responsibility of the partner insurance company. Customers are advised to read the product brochure and policy document carefully before purchasing. Free-look period of 30 days is available on all life insurance products.
