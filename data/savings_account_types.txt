SAVINGS ACCOUNT TYPES — PRODUCT DISCLOSURE DOCUMENT

This document outlines the savings account variants offered by the Bank and is intended to assist customers in selecting the most appropriate product for their financial needs.

REGULAR SAVINGS ACCOUNT
The Regular Savings Account is the Bank's standard deposit product for individual customers. It requires a minimum monthly average balance (MAB) of ₹5,000 for urban and semi-urban branches, and ₹2,000 for rural branches. Failure to maintain the MAB attracts a non-maintenance charge of ₹200 per month for urban accounts and ₹100 per month for rural accounts. Interest is credited at 3.50% per annum, calculated on the daily closing balance and paid quarterly. A free RuPay Classic debit card is issued upon account opening. Customers are entitled to five free cash withdrawals per month at any branch.

PREMIUM SAVINGS ACCOUNT
The Premium Savings Account is designed for customers who maintain higher balances and require enhanced service levels. The minimum MAB requirement is ₹25,000. Account holders receive a complimentary Visa Platinum debit card with a daily ATM withdrawal limit of ₹1,00,000 and a point-of-sale limit of ₹2,00,000. Interest accrues at 4.00% per annum on daily closing balances. Premium account holders are assigned a dedicated relationship manager and receive priority banking services at designated branches. Complimentary demand drafts (up to five per month) and NEFT transactions (unlimited) are included. Non-maintenance charges are ₹500 per month.

SALARY SAVINGS ACCOUNT
Offered exclusively to salaried employees of organisations with a registered corporate relationship with the Bank. Zero minimum balance requirement; however, the account converts to a Regular Savings Account if no salary credit is received for three consecutive months. Includes personal accident insurance cover of ₹2,00,000 and air accident cover of ₹10,00,000 at no additional cost. Free unlimited ATM withdrawals at any Bank ATM and five free withdrawals per month at other bank ATMs.

SENIOR CITIZEN SAVINGS ACCOUNT
Designed for customers aged 60 years and above. Minimum MAB of ₹1,000 only. Interest rate is 0.50% per annum above the prevailing Regular Savings rate (currently 4.00% per annum). Dedicated senior citizen banking counters are available at all branches. Doorstep banking services are available upon request for customers aged 70 and above.

WOMEN'S SAVINGS ACCOUNT
A specialised product for women customers. Minimum MAB of ₹5,000. Includes complimentary personal accident cover of ₹1,00,000 and a free locker facility (subject to availability) for the first year. Preferential interest rates on fixed deposits opened against this account.

MINOR SAVINGS ACCOUNT (PEHLA KADAM / PEHLI UDAAN)
Pehla Kadam is available for minors of any age (operated jointly with a parent/guardian). Pehli Udaan is available for minors aged 10 years and above who can operate the account independently. Both variants have zero minimum balance requirements. A personalised cheque book and a photo debit card are issued. Daily transaction limit is capped at ₹5,000 for safety.

Note: All interest rates are subject to revision by the Bank's Asset Liability Management Committee (ALCO) without prior notice. Customers are advised to verify current rates at any branch or via the Bank's official website before making financial decisions.
