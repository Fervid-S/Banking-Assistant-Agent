PERSONAL LOAN — PRODUCT FEATURES, ELIGIBILITY, AND CHARGES

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
The Bank's Personal Loan is an unsecured retail loan product designed to meet customers' immediate financial needs including medical expenses, home renovation, travel, education-related expenditure, and debt consolidation. No collateral or guarantor is required for loans below the prescribed threshold. Loan proceeds are disbursed directly to the applicant's Bank account, typically within 48 hours of document verification and sanction.

ELIGIBILITY

Salaried Applicants:
  • Age: 21 to 60 years (at loan maturity).
  • Minimum monthly net take-home salary: ₹20,000 (metro/urban centres); ₹15,000 (semi-urban/rural).
  • Employment: Permanent/confirmed employees of Central/State Government, PSU, listed corporates, or corporates on the Bank's approved list.
  • Minimum employment tenure: 1 year total; minimum 6 months with the current employer.
  • CIBIL score: Minimum 700.

Self-Employed Professionals (Doctors, CAs, Architects, etc.):
  • Age: 25 to 65 years (at loan maturity).
  • Minimum net annual income: ₹4,00,000 (as per last 2 years' ITR).
  • Minimum practice/business vintage: 2 years.
  • CIBIL score: Minimum 700.

LOAN PARAMETERS
  • Minimum loan amount: ₹50,000.
  • Maximum loan amount:
      – Salaried: ₹20,00,000 or 18 times monthly net salary, whichever is lower.
      – Self-employed: ₹15,00,000 or 2 times annual net income, whichever is lower.
  • Maximum tenure: 60 months (salaried); 48 months (self-employed).

INTEREST RATES (FLOATING, LINKED TO RLLR)
Rates are subject to revision based on RBI policy actions affecting the Repo rate:
  • CIBIL ≥ 800: 11.50% per annum.
  • CIBIL 750–799: 12.50% per annum.
  • CIBIL 700–749: 13.50% per annum.

Rates for self-employed professionals are 0.50% per annum higher than the corresponding salaried applicant rates across all CIBIL bands.

PROCESSING AND OTHER CHARGES
  • Processing fee: 2% of the loan amount, minimum ₹1,500, maximum ₹20,000. Deducted upfront from the disbursed amount.
  • Prepayment/part prepayment: Permitted after completion of 12 EMIs. Foreclosure charge: 4% of outstanding principal within 12–24 months of disbursal; 2% thereafter.
  • Cheque/NACH bounce charge: ₹500 per instance.
  • Late payment interest: 2% per month on overdue EMI amount.
  • Duplicate NOC/statement issuance: ₹250 per document.
  • EMI date change: Permitted once during the loan tenure; charge ₹300.

DOCUMENTATION REQUIRED (SALARIED)
  • Signed application form.
  • PAN card, Aadhaar card.
  • Last 3 months' salary slips.
  • Last 6 months' bank statement (salary account).
  • Form 16 (most recent year).

DISBURSEMENT TIMELINE
Post receipt of complete documentation: Credit appraisal within 24 hours; disbursal to savings account within 48 hours. Applicants with a pre-approved Personal Loan offer (communicated via net banking or mobile app) may receive instant disbursement.

PRE-APPROVED PERSONAL LOANS
Existing customers with a satisfactory relationship history may be pre-approved for Personal Loans. Such offers are communicated through Net Banking, the Mobile Banking App, and SMS. Pre-approved loans require minimal documentation (Aadhaar-based e-KYC may suffice) and carry the best applicable interest rate for the customer's CIBIL band.
