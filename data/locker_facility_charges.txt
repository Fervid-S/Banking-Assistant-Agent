SAFE DEPOSIT LOCKER FACILITY — ALLOTMENT POLICY, CHARGES, AND CONDITIONS

OVERVIEW
The Bank provides Safe Deposit Locker (SDL) facilities at designated branches for the safekeeping of jewellery, title deeds, important documents, and other valuables. The Bank does not insure the contents of lockers and assumes no liability for their safety except as mandated by the Reserve Bank of India's revised SDL guidelines effective January 1, 2023.

ELIGIBILITY FOR ALLOTMENT
  • Any individual resident customer maintaining a savings or current account with the Bank may apply.
  • HUFs, clubs, associations, and companies with a current account relationship are also eligible.
  • Locker facility is not available to accounts classified as inoperative or dormant.
  • A minimum Fixed Deposit equivalent to three years' locker rent must be maintained as security deposit at the time of allotment. This FD is held under lien and earns interest at the applicable FD rate.

LOCKER SIZES AND ANNUAL RENTAL CHARGES

  Small Locker (Internal Dimensions: 4" × 4" × 12"):
    • Metro/Urban branches: ₹2,000 per annum.
    • Semi-urban branches: ₹1,500 per annum.
    • Rural branches: ₹1,000 per annum.

  Medium Locker (Internal Dimensions: 6" × 6" × 12"):
    • Metro/Urban: ₹3,500 per annum.
    • Semi-urban: ₹2,500 per annum.
    • Rural: ₹1,800 per annum.

  Large Locker (Internal Dimensions: 8" × 10" × 12"):
    • Metro/Urban: ₹6,000 per annum.
    • Semi-urban: ₹4,500 per annum.
    • Rural: ₹3,000 per annum.

  Extra Large Locker (Internal Dimensions: 10" × 18" × 12"):
    • Metro/Urban: ₹9,000 per annum.
    • Semi-urban: ₹7,000 per annum.
    • Rural: ₹5,000 per annum.

  Rental charges include GST and are payable annually in advance. Unpaid rental for 3 or more years constitutes grounds for forced opening of the locker.

OPERATING HOURS
  • Locker access is available during the Bank's working hours (typically 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM on working days).
  • Saturday hours: 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM (where applicable).
  • Access is not permitted on bank holidays, Sundays, and second/fourth Saturdays.

NOMINATION
  • Locker holders are strongly encouraged to register a nominee. In the event of the death of the sole locker holder, the nominee may access the locker subject to production of the death certificate and completing the required formalities.
  • Joint locker accounts may operate on an "Either or Survivor" or "Jointly" basis; the operating instruction must be specified at the time of account opening.

LOST KEY
  • In the event of loss of the locker key, the customer must immediately inform the branch in writing.
  • A duplicate key request triggers a forced opening procedure: the locker is broken open by a certified locksmith in the presence of a Bank officer and the customer (or their authorised representative).
  • Forced opening charges: ₹2,000 (small/medium lockers); ₹3,500 (large/extra-large lockers). Cost of new lock is additional.

LIABILITY FRAMEWORK (AS PER RBI GUIDELINES)
  • In cases of loss of locker contents due to fire, theft, burglary, dacoity, or building collapse attributable to the Bank's own negligence, the Bank's liability is capped at 100 times the prevailing annual rental of the locker.
  • The Bank is not liable for losses arising from natural calamities (floods, earthquakes), acts of war, or negligence of the locker holder (e.g., leaving the locker unattended during access).
