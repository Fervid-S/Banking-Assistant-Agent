CURRENT ACCOUNT VARIANTS — PRODUCT DISCLOSURE DOCUMENT

Current Accounts are non-interest-bearing transactional accounts intended for business entities, self-employed professionals, and traders who require high-frequency banking operations. The Bank does not pay interest on credit balances held in Current Accounts.

BASIC CURRENT ACCOUNT
Minimum quarterly average balance (QAB): ₹10,000. Suitable for small businesses, sole proprietorships, and startups. Includes a free MICR cheque book of 50 leaves per quarter. Cash deposit limit at the base branch: ₹3,00,000 per month free of charge; excess cash deposits are charged at ₹2 per ₹1,000 (minimum ₹50). Non-maintenance charge: ₹500 per quarter. Overdraft facility not included by default; may be applied for separately.

BUSINESS CURRENT ACCOUNT
Minimum QAB: ₹50,000. Intended for medium-scale enterprises and partnership firms. Free cash deposit limit is ₹10,00,000 per month. Includes unlimited NEFT/RTGS transactions initiated through the Bank's Net Banking portal. A dedicated business relationship manager is assigned to accounts maintaining a QAB above ₹2,00,000. Non-maintenance charge: ₹1,500 per quarter.

PREMIUM BUSINESS CURRENT ACCOUNT
Minimum QAB: ₹2,00,000. Designed for high-turnover businesses, corporate entities, and large partnerships. Free cash deposit: unlimited at base branch. Includes a payable-at-par (PAP) cheque book of 200 leaves per month, free demand drafts, and multi-city banking. A sweep-in facility can be configured so that surplus funds above a defined threshold are automatically transferred to an FD, earning term deposit interest rates. Non-maintenance charge: ₹3,000 per quarter.

TRADE CURRENT ACCOUNT
Specifically designed for exporters and importers. Minimum QAB: ₹1,00,000. Provides access to export bill negotiation, letter of credit (LC) issuance, and foreign currency demand drafts. Charges for trade finance instruments are reduced by 25% compared to standard schedule-of-charges rates. Forex advisory services are available from the Bank's treasury desk on request.

STAFF CURRENT ACCOUNT
Available exclusively to confirmed employees of the Bank. No minimum balance requirement. Includes five free RTGS and ten free NEFT transactions per month.

OVERDRAFT AGAINST CURRENT ACCOUNT
Eligible Current Account holders may apply for a sanctioned overdraft facility. The overdraft limit is assessed based on the average quarterly credit turnover, typically up to 50% of the average monthly turnover subject to a maximum of ₹25,00,000. Interest on the utilised overdraft is charged at the Bank's prevailing base rate plus a spread (currently Base Rate + 3.50% per annum), applied on a daily product basis.

IMPORTANT OPERATING CONDITIONS
Accounts that remain inoperative (no debit or credit transactions, excluding bank-initiated charges) for 24 consecutive months are classified as dormant. Reactivation requires a written request submitted at the home branch along with valid KYC documents.
